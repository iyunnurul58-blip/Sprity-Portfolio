Digital Opportunity Shop

Sprity: Declutter with Purpose, Shop as Opportunity

Sprity is a digital opportunity shop, a platform that turns donated preloved goods into social impact. We collect unused clothing, sanitize and verify every piece, and resell it through a curated catalogue to transparently fund partner charities.

📅 Duration: Sep 2026 – Dec 2026
🎯 Role: Product Manager

Problem & Current Situation

📦 Supply Friction (Donors)

The Hassle: Donors have overflowing wardrobes but lack time and energy to sort, clean, photograph, and list items.

Logistical Bottleneck: 45% do not know where to donate and 45% find the manual handover process too demanding.

Impact: Wearable clothing sits idle in closets for months without social utility.

🛒 Demand Hesitation (Thrift Buyers)

Quality Uncertainty: 75% of buyers prioritize cleanliness and condition above all else.

Sizing & Hygiene Fears: 55% worry about unhygienic preloved goods or inaccurate sizing.

Trust Deficit: 65% require public receipts and verified audit logs to trust donation platforms.

🔴 Problem Statement

Decluttering is stalled by high operational friction for donors, while potential buyers hesitate due to hygiene, sizing, and transparency concerns. Sprity bridges this gap with a fully managed pickup, verification, and add social value by providing transparent charity funding loop.

💡 Implemented Managed Solutions

  • Dual Inbound Channels: Doorstep pickup in Jakarta or drop-off at HQ.
  • Quality & Hygiene Guarantee: Every garment is sanitized, QC-graded, and measured.
  • Standardized Pricing: Transparent tiers averaging IDR 50,000 per garment.
  • Public Audit Hub: Publicly accessible audit log containing transaction IDs and disbursement receipts.

📈 Risk Management & MVP Mitigation

  • Image Hotlinking / CORS: Mitigated via standardized direct-rendering URLs.
  • API Endpoint Stability: Resolved using central URL variables and strict release checks.
  • Pickup Logistics Costs: Managed with a 10-garment minimum threshold and HQ drop-off incentives.
  • Garment Quality Assurance: Low-grade pieces routed directly to textile recycling partners.
